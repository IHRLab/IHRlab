Publications & Outputs

Selected peer-reviewed work from the IHR Lab and our collaborators.

Peer-Reviewed

DT4PCP: A Digital Twin Framework for Personalized Care Planning Applied to Type-2 Diabetes Management
Javad Mohammad Alizadeh, Mukesh Kumar Patel, Huanmei Wu (2025).

We introduce a general digital-twin framework for personalized care planning (DT4PCP), built around a real-time virtual representation of a patient's health and predictive models that enable adaptive, personalized care. Implemented for type-2 diabetes, DT4PCP-T2D collects behavioral data in real time, predicts emergency-department risk, simulates intervention effects, and personalizes care strategies to reduce ED visits.

Predicting Emergency-Department Visits for Patients with Type-II Diabetes
Javad M. Alizadeh, Jay S. Patel, Gabriel Tajeu, Yuzhou Chen, Ilene L. Hollin, Mukesh K. Patel, Junchao Fei, Huanmei Wu (2024).

This study develops and validates machine-learning models to estimate emergency-department visits among patients with type-II diabetes. Ensemble Learning and Random Forest models demonstrated superior performance in discrimination, calibration, and clinical applicability.

The Current Landscape of Cybersecurity Training in CAHIIM-Accredited Programs
Huanmei Wu, Mukesh Kumar Patel, Chiu C. Tan (2024).

Using natural language processing, this project maps the current landscape of cybersecurity training in CAHIIM-accredited health-information-management and health-informatics programs, identifying trends in how privacy and security are integrated into academic curricula.

The Landscape of Health-Informatics Education in Low- or Middle-Income Countries
Jay S. Patel, Omotese Oaikhena, Thanh TD Phan, Hoa Vo, Javad M. Alizadeh, Huanmei Wu (2023).

This study characterizes the state of health-informatics education in low- and middle-income countries, focusing on Africa and Southeast Asia, and finds that most such education exists in the USA while comparatively little is available elsewhere.

Social Determinants of Health & Emergency-Department Visits in Diabetes and Hypertension
Javad M. Alizadeh, Huanmei Wu, Jay S. Patel (2023).

This work explores the influence of social determinants of health on emergency-department visits among patients with diabetes and hypertension, contributing to an understanding of healthcare-utilization patterns among people with chronic disease.
